Has your account turned up in a data breach?

Check an email address against known breaches, or test whether a password appears in leaked password lists. Your password never leaves this device in full.

Breach and password data come from the public Have I Been Pwned APIs, by Troy Hunt, licensed CC BY 4.0. Passwords are checked with k-anonymity: only the first 5 characters of the SHA-1 hash are sent, and the match happens on your device.

A clean result covers only breaches that have been collected. It does not prove an account is safe.
